Starter and up

Included in every build

  • Google Business Profile claimed, verified, and completed (two verification attempts included). Google Analytics and Search Console set up, consent-gated, and handed to you.
  • A contact form with spam protection, hours and location structured for search, built for phones first and measured for speed before it goes live.
  • Your domain, in your name, at your registrar. Hosting on your own Cloudflare account, paid by you — usually $0. We point the domain at the email provider you choose; we don't provide email.
  • One design review and one revision round. Changes after approval are Care edits or new work. The timeline is set in the proposal.
  • You supply the photos and the facts. We send a rough draft of the page text; you finish it. We don't shoot photos or write finished copy (we can refer a photographer). Content more than 30 days late may pause and re-slot the project.
  • Every tool the site relies on is yours — booking engine, Square, Shopify — in your name, paid by you, listed in the proposal. Medical, med-spa, and dispensary sites run with marketing tracking switched off entirely.
  • You own it. Domain, hosting account, and every third-party account are yours from day one; the site itself once it's paid off. Not offered: social posting, review replies, ad management, email hosting, rush timelines.

Same price either way. No carry, no fee, no interest. The Starter is standard only. A plan sale includes Maintenance or higher for the first 12 months, billed separately from go-live.

At $2,400 the plan is $600 + $600, then $200 a month for six months; at $3,600 it's $900 + $900, then $300. The monthly payment is always the price divided by twelve.

Holding the site

  • Standard: paid in full before go-live, so you own the site at go-live.
  • Plan: the domain and third-party accounts are yours from day one; we hold the hosting account until the final installment clears, then transfer it within five business days.
  • Missed installment: a reminder at 10 days; at 30 days the site comes down and Care billing pauses; at 60 days all work stops until the balance is paid. No reinstatement fee. You keep the domain and your accounts throughout.

Also

  • Pass-through costs (registrar and domain-restore fees, your subscriptions) are yours, paid at signing or to the vendor, never financed. The signing payment is non-refundable once work begins. New work during a plan is quoted and paid on its own terms. Extras and Launch Setup don't credit toward an upgrade.
  • At signing we'll ask one question: can we write this build up as a technical case study and show the live site? Yes unless you say no, and no is fine; you see it before it's published. Everything here is set out in full in the sales agreement you sign.
