Shared rules: `b2-common.md`. Brief items 3, 4, 5 and 9; decisions D4 (c)
and D8.

1. **Dark paper, (c) the hybrid** (D4), lifted from
   `../proofs/cc-dark-paper.md`: the mounted herbarium sheets (Home hero,
   About) become a dimmed-cream board with a visible edge; brown cards,
   notes and the letter stay. Founder change (call 7): the tapes on the
   cream boards use the light scheme's tape tokens (the dark ones read as
   heavy bands there). Fix the a5 minors as you lift (specificity of the
   letter rule, the contrast numbers recorded per surface).
2. **Specimens pressed flat** (item 3): no float shadow on specimens on
   paper (a hairline contact shadow at most); the hero sprigs held by gum
   strips, placed on bare stem, never over blooms (the proof's hit test);
   petal fills on mounted sheets 8 to 12% less saturated; loose sprigs on
   the cloth keep a very soft contact shadow, none floats.
3. **The dark cloth** (item 5): the two-thread sage cross in dark at
   **0.14** (call 7: at 0.18 the lit butter note read sunk into the check);
   the dashed SVG gone or a faint running stitch over the check; a 1px
   lamp-lit rim on the valance and hem scallops. Check the butter note now
   reads as lit paper on the cloth, not a hole.
4. **Writing on the lines** (item 4): whole-pixel pitches (`--lh` 30 or
   32px), paragraph margins in whole lines, rules in the same rotated box as
   the text, positioned from the baseline, on the Home cards, the Services
   recipe cards, the About letter and the Contact textarea. The last line of
   each block sits on its rule like the first, at every size.
5. **Hide the repeats** (item 9): the torn edge from a tile of 900px or
   more, or three variants per instance; three or four tape clip-path
   variants and a per-instance `--tape-w`.
6. **One indigo thread** (D8): exactly one indigo-and-white gingham tape,
   no text on it.
