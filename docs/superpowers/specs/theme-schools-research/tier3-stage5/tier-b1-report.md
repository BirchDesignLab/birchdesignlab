## Calls for you

1. **The chinoiserie trunks** (controller). The birches bend in long S
   curves, so they read rubbery rather than as birch. **Recommend:
   straighter trunks** with a slight lean and the odd kink, as birch grows.
2. **The alphabet band** (critic). Thin pale-blue crosses at 14rem: it reads
   like a pixel font, not stitching. **Recommend:** oxblood thread, larger,
   with each cross visible as an X.
3. **The dark founder room** (critic). In dark, the founder's band and the
   hero wall share one ground, so the letter has no room of its own.
   **Recommend:** a deeper lacquer green behind the founder band in dark.
4. **The dark lacquer yellow** (critic). In dark, Services' process band
   reads muddy olive-brown, and the cameos' rings make halos on the band in
   both schemes. **Recommend:** a warmer, clearer ochre in dark, and rings
   in the band's own colour.
5. **The footer's blue band in dark** (critic). A pale porcelain line under
   the oxblood valance. **Recommend:** a deep porcelain blue in dark.
6. **The No. 2 cutting on the toile wall** (critic). The swatch is the same
   oxblood toile as the wall, so its overhang melts into it. **Recommend:**
   the No. 2 cutting in porcelain-blue ink (the proof's other ink), so it
   reads as a cutting laid on the papered wall.
7. **The light envelope flap** (critic). The right darkness, but a cool
   grey. **Recommend:** the same darkness in a warm paper hue.
8. **The Services rosette** (controller). It reads as a red starburst.
   **Recommend:** softer, fewer pleats around a covered button, like a
   ribbon rosette.
