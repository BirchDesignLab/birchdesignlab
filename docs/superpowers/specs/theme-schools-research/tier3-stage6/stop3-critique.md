## Cottagecore and grandmillennial

**C1, phone seeds.** As written it cannot be built, or it brings the bug
back: the smallest seed needs about 60px clear with its margin and the
phone gutter is about 14px (the sliver CC3 removed); the seed canvas sits
under `main`, so "over the sheet's margin" means a second layer above the
paper, which would cross text. The fix overshot: at Stage 5 phone seeds
showed in 97 to 100% of frames and the only complaint was wedging; now
About shows 0%. The before frames show where seeds read well on a phone:
the open cloth right of the wordmark. **Verdict:** on phones only, a
smaller seed size may seat in the header cloth beside the wordmark and under
the nav, still under the paper; 1 or 2 seeds; nothing over the sheet.

**C2, tablet Home band.** Close to the "keep two columns" option passed
over at CC6, and the band grows with width (about 60px at 641, 180px at
820), so a side column just moves the gap under the sheet. But 744, 768,
810 and 820 (portrait iPads) all sit in this range. **Verdict:** keep,
built as a wrap: the sheet floats, the subline starts beside it under the
lead and runs full width below; check 641 for single-word lines.

**G1, Home frame.** For the cap: 352 is the approved desktop size; the
other reading makes desktop 384 too. Against it: the jump it fixed was seen
only by someone dragging a window across 1100; the cost is permanent for
every landscape tablet (1024, 1080), a smaller frame and a hero about 40px
shorter, and at 1440 there is plenty of wall for 384. **Verdict:** 384 from
about 873 up, all widths; check plate clearance at 1101 and 1180.

**G2, picots.** The edge reads as a scallop, which the school's other trims
already use. But loops outside the band would be 2 to 3px at 1x and read as
fuzz; today's edge reads as deliberate trim at 1440 and 390, both schemes.
**Verdict:** keep; fold the lone "A" at 390 into the fix task.

## Vaporwave

**V1, the drum's Venus.** There is no other classical still: the repo has
one figure (the Chiragan Venus scan); every other still is a column or a
sphere, and a new figure is a download, licence check and processing job.
The repeat is mostly not new either: Home's desktop kiosk showed this Venus
when "Venus on a drum" was approved; V3 only added it on phones. **Verdict:**
re-render the same Venus in pink marble on a white drum, turned the other
way, through the existing render pipeline (tint and turn already supported):
a second cast in the same mall, no new art.

**V2, phone kiosk props.** "In front" has no floor at 390 (the stand fills
the width); 64px for both makes a 64px ball beside a 33px-wide bust; bigger
props crowd the plaque, which becomes wave 3's elevator. The diagnosis
holds: at 390 both are about 24px wide, sitting on the plaque's bottom edge
like dust. **Verdict:** Venus about 60px tall, sphere about 44px across,
bases dropped to the stand's foot line, clearance on the plaque and button
re-checked.

## Bauhaus and swiss

**B1, the hover block.** "Down and right" cannot clear a beam the square
stands on: down sinks the block further in. The build already does the
classic hard shadow (the square lifts up and left, the block stays at
rest), and at 1440 the lift reads; only an 8px strip merges into a 45px
beam, hover only, desktop only. The real defect is the minor beside it:
during the 440 ms pick-me beat the square ignores hover and press, the very
moment it invites a grab. **Verdict:** keep as built; fix the beat's dead
zone (wave 2 deferred minor C1). If the lift should read more, raise this
square's lift to 6 to 8px (breaks the brief's 4px: founder's call).

**S1, the foot rule.** The code justifies it as a crop rule, and past the
image it crops nothing. But the trunk is a red and white duotone on red, so
the image's left edge cannot be seen; a rule stopping there would end
mid-red for no visible reason. **Verdict:** keep.

**S2, hairlines.** Home's rule vanishes under the photo's black foot, and a
1px black line on red is faint on phones. But Home's matches the specimen
rules just below it, and About's reads cleanly at 820. **Verdict:** keep.
