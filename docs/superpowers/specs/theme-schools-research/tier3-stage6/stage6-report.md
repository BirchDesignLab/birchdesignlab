## 1. What Stage 6 was

Stage 6 as defined in `tier3-briefs/portal.md` section 6: regenerate the
sheets and arrival strips, re-run the set critique, check the boundaries
(the confusable pairs at thumbnail scale, in dark and on phones), build
Stage 4's parked interactables (the swiss width dial and the bauhaus
questionnaire card), then open the transitions phase.

The plan (`stage6-plan.md`) had one wave and three stops. The stop 1 panel
sized the work at about 20 tasks and 220 to 260 agents, and the founder took
three waves with a stop after each (stop 1, call 11); stop 4 added a closing
wave, and stop 5 a final-fix task. As run:

- **Tier A** (11-19): A0 evidence by script (captures of every school, five
  pages, both schemes, desktop, tablet and phone; arrival strips; boundary
  sheets; the bauhaus assemblies side by side), then the A1 panel: six
  school critics, a glass drag diagnosis, a Stage 5 ledger triage, a set
  critic who drafted stop 1, a claim check. **Stop 1.**
- **Wave 1** (tasks 1 to 8): the two laggards (glass, swiss), the two parked
  toys, two proofs (glass R2, swiss P3). **Stop 2.**
- **Wave 2** (tasks 9 to 17): the stop 2 answers, the four strong schools,
  S8 (every school re-expresses "how a project runs"), the hover guard.
  **Stop 3.**
- **Wave 3** (tasks 18 to 27): two fix tasks, then the new interactables,
  cheap ones first, the big three last. **Stop 4.**
- **Wave 4** (tasks 28 to 30): the closing wave, building the stop 4
  verdicts and wave 3's deferred minors. **Stop 5.**
- **Task 31**: the stop 5 verdicts, one task with its review.

**The schools.** The six tranche-1 schools were re-reviewed and built on:
vaporwave, grandmillennial, glassmorphism, cottagecore, bauhaus and swiss.
The seventh, quiet (the root site's school), was not re-reviewed; it
changed only through the hover gating (section 3).
