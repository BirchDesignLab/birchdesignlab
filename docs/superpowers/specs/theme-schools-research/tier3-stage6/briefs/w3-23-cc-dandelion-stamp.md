# W3 task 23: cottagecore, the dandelion clock and the date stamp

Shared rules: `w3-common.md`. Source: `../a1-critic-cottagecore.md`
("Interactables" 1 and 3), stop 1 call 7. Wave 2 task 16 already made a tap
in dark call a firefly; this task adds the dandelion's light half and the
stamp.

1. **Blow the dandelion clock (light).** A dried dandelion clock lies on the
   cloth: Home's closing band beside the lavender, and Sent. A tap or long
   press releases 6 to 10 seeds into the existing seed canvas (`fx.ts`),
   which drift like the rest; the clock shows a bald patch that refills
   over about 20 s. In dark the same spot is where a tapped firefly rises
   (reuse wave 2's tap-to-call). Inert (`aria-hidden`), pointer and touch;
   the new seeds obey the seat rules wave 2 and stop 3 set.
2. **The rubber stamp (Contact and Sent).** A wooden-handled date stamp
   beside the postcard thunks down on tap: a fresh cancel mark at a random
   angle and ink density, same 09-23-26 date and ring as today's postmark;
   up to about five overlap, the oldest fading. On Sent, one press cancels
   the stamp afresh. No new words. Inert; never over the Contact form or its
   labels.
