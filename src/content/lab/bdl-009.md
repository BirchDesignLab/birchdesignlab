## The menu, honestly

Every dish carries its real price, its real dietary tags, and where a plate is
priced by the morning's catch, no price at all, just "Market Price, call to
order," because a fixed number would be a lie the kitchen never told. Every
dish on the page is marked with what it actually is.

A dish offered two ways prices both ways, cleanly, rather than as an asterisk
hung off a single number. A price is one of three honest shapes: a flat
figure when the kitchen knows it, a set of named variants when the dish comes
in more than one size, and an admission when the number genuinely is not
knowable that morning. Most restaurant sites on this coast pick a fourth
shape, which is a photograph of a chalkboard, or a PDF from 2019.

## What a search engine sees

The menu is not a picture of a menu and not a download. It is real markup,
which means a search engine reads the business, its hours, the menu, each
section, and each dish as structured facts rather than inferring them from a
photograph. That is the difference between a listing that shows the food and
a blue link that shows the domain name.
