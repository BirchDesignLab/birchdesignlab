Most restaurant menus on this coast live online as a photograph of a
chalkboard or a PDF from 2019. You pinch, you zoom, and the prices are
three years old.

Bayou Kitchen is a restaurant we made up, in Gulfport, with a real site
built for it. There is no client. This page argues one thing: the menu.

## Three honest prices

A dish with one price shows it. A dish that comes two ways prices both:
half dozen, dozen. And when the price depends on what the boats brought in
that morning, the menu says "Market Price" and asks you to call, because
any number printed there would be wrong by lunch.

Dietary tags sit on the dish they describe: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free,
raw, spicy.

## Text, not a picture

The menu is typed, not photographed. A phone reads it without zooming, and
a search engine sees the restaurant, its hours, each section and each dish
as facts instead of guessing at them from an image.

## What is not here yet

The site has an order page, and it is not taking orders yet. We will write
that half when it does.
