Every color and typeface on this site earned its place in a tuning room:
candidates lined up, contrast ratios computed live, bark redrawn a
hundred ways until something worked. This specimen is that room with the
door propped open. The full instrument stays on the workbench; the
controls that prove interesting graduate here.

**How it works:** the page injects candidate design tokens as scoped CSS
and recomputes WCAG contrast ratios on every change. The bark panel
drives the same seeded pattern core and WebGL renderer that grow the
bark on every other page of this site.
