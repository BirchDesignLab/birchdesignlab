This one started as a favor. The owners of
[Cheer and Chatter Social Club](/lab/bdl-005/) had a website, a box office, a
mailing list and a live game app, all working, and no way to picture how the
four fit together. So we drew it for them: every place their information
lives, every line it travels, in their words instead of ours.

It turned out to be the clearest account of the build we had. The version
here is the same map, re-addressed to you instead of to them, with the
private details taken back out. The switch above the map trades the plain
labels for the engineering ones, so the same drawing answers both "what does
it do" and "how is it made".

Nothing on the map is a mock-up of a plan. Every box is running, and every
line is something we wrote so four separate services behave like one
business. The ticket desk and the bingo caller further down are miniatures
you can drive; the real ones are described in [BDL-005](/lab/bdl-005/).
