Instruments before opinions. Every value on this site was argued into place
by hand, and the arguments are hard to reopen once the hex is in the
stylesheet. The Regulator is the bench where they stay reopenable: a color
ramp that solves itself against contrast targets instead of taste, a spacing
switch that reports what the system is already doing, and a crown that
retunes the bark of a real page while you watch.

Three controls, three natures, and the difference between them is the point.
Color spins free, because hue is a circle with no ends to hit. Spacing steps
into detents, because there are three answers and you are picking one. Grain
pulls out to tune and pushes in to set, because changing a default is not the
same gesture as looking at it.

> The rule the whole panel obeys: green means running. The crown stem turns
> accent green while it is pulled out and goes dark the moment it is pushed
> back in. If a green pixel does not map to something actually happening, the
> instrument is lying.

Setting the crown does not write anything. It prints the diff — every line
that would have to change, including the ones easy to forget, like the
fallback literal in the field component and the OG images that quietly take
the pattern default. Paste it if you mean it.
