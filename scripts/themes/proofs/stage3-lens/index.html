Glass that bends the room

Drag the lens across the wallpaper. The panes bend what sits behind them where the browser allows it and frost it everywhere else.

What sits behind

The wallpaper holds still while the orbs drift at their own pace, so the colour under each pane keeps changing as you read.

The lens draws that backdrop itself, so it bends the same way on every browser. Text is not part of the backdrop it draws.

Scroll back up and throw the lens. It glides, slows and stops against the edge of the window.
