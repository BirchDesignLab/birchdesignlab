What happened2 / 4

Two critical flaws in PHP itself.

On October 21, PHP shipped emergency fixes for two flaws researchers named PHP2Shell.

On an unpatched server, under the right conditions, an attacker can run their own code without logging in.

PHP runs roughly 7 in 10 websites where the language can be measured. Every WordPress site is one of them.
