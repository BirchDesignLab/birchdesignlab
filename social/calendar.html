Instagram

If your website runs on WordPress, this may have been a long week.

On October 21, PHP shipped emergency fixes for two critical flaws researchers are calling PHP2Shell. PHP runs roughly 7 in 10 websites where the language can be measured, every WordPress site included. On an unpatched server, under the right conditions, someone can run their own code on it without logging in.

Nothing Birch Design Lab has built runs on PHP, and nothing we build will. Our sites and software are written in TypeScript and served from Cloudflare's network. There's no PHP in the stack for this to reach.

PHP has been a running joke among developers for about twenty years. There's a well-known 2012 essay titled "PHP: a fractal of bad design." To be fair, modern PHP is better than its reputation. We'd still rather not have it on the list of things to patch.

The bigger lesson is about off-the-shelf sites. WordPress gets its features from plugins, and plugins are where about 9 in 10 of its security holes turn up. Wix and Squarespace sites are reportedly in the clear this time, since those platforms don't run PHP. The trade is that the platform keeps the site. A Wix site can't leave Wix.

A custom build is code you own and can move, with fewer moving parts, built around how your business actually runs.

On WordPress and not sure you're patched? Ask your host one question: is our PHP on the October 21 release? Updating WordPress or its plugins doesn't cover this one. If you'd like a second pair of eyes, send us a message. No pitch.

We build custom websites, and we'll still tell you when you don't need one.

If you need something live this week, a template already looks like your business, and the site's whole job is your hours, your number and a map, Wix or Squarespace is a fine answer. Keep the money.
